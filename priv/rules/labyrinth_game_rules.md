# Rules of the "Labyrinth" Game (with Game Master)

The classic paper-and-pencil verbal logic game **"Labyrinth"** (also known as "Hunt for the Minotaur" or "Paper Labyrinth") is a tactical, turn-based exploration game for 2 to 6+ players guided by a **Game Master (GM)**.

---

## 🎯 Objective
Players explore a hidden, unseen maze to locate the **treasure** and escape through the **exit** while avoiding traps, monsters, and rival explorers. Only the **Game Master sees the full map** — players navigate blindly, mapping the layout step-by-step based on the GM's feedback.

---

## 🛠 Setup & Preparation

1. **Role of the Game Master (GM):**
   * Draws the secret master map on a grid sheet (typically $10 \times 10$ to $20 \times 20$ cells).
   * Marks internal walls (borders between cells).
   * Places key game elements:
     * 🚪 **Entrances / Exits** (1 or more along the perimeter).
     * 💎 **Treasure / Relic** (primary objective).
     * 🏥 **Hospital / Medical Sanctuary** (heals Wounded players back to 3/3 HP).
     * ⚔️ **Arsenal / Armory** (fully reloads Bullets 3/3 and Grenades 3/3).
     * 🕳 **Pits / Traps** (causes falling, lost turns, or disorientation).
     * 🌀 **Teleporters / Wormholes** (paired connected cells).
     * 👹 **Monster / Minotaur** (wandering monster hunting explorers).
2. **Players:**
   * Each player begins with **3 HP** (`Healthy 🤠`), **3 Bullets (`3/3 🔫`)**, and **3 Grenades (`3/3 💣`)**.
   * Players take notes and map out the maze as they explore.

---

## 🚶‍♂️ Gameplay

Players take turns in sequence. On their turn, a player declares a direction (**North ▲**, **South ▼**, **West ◄**, **East ►**) and an action mode:

1. **🚶 Move:** Step 1 cell in the declared direction.
2. **🎯 Shoot:** Fire a ranged gunshot up to 3 cells in a straight line.
3. **💣 Grenade:** Throw an explosive grenade to **demolish an internal wall segment**, creating a new corridor! *(Note: Outer perimeter boundary walls surrounding the whole labyrinth are indestructible).*
4. **⏩ Pass:** Skip current turn.

---

## 📢 Game Master Responses & Landings

| Situation | GM Response | Action / Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clear path** | *"Step"* / *"Moved"* | The player successfully moves to the target cell. |
| **Wall** | *"Wall"* / *"Bump"* | The player stays in place; turn ends. |
| **Hospital 🏥** | *"Visited Hospital"* | Restores a **Wounded 🩸** player back to **Healthy 🤠 (3/3 HP)**. |
| **Arsenal ⚔️** | *"Visited Arsenal"* | Fully reloads **Bullets (3/3 🔫)** and **Grenades (3/3 💣)**. |
| **Wall Demolished 💥** | *"BOOM! Wall Demolished"* | Grenade destroys target internal wall segment. |
| **Trap / Pit 🕳** | *"Pit"* | Player falls into pit; loses next turn (`Stunned`). Creates Disorientation Post-It. |
| **Teleport 🌀** | *"Teleport"* | Warps player to paired portal cell. Creates Disorientation Post-It. |
| **Treasure 💎** | *"Found treasure!"* | The player picks up the treasure (objective becomes escaping). |
| **Encounter** | *"Gunshot / Explosions"* | Auditory echoes propagate to nearby players within 3 cells. |

---

## ⚔️ Health, Wounded Status & Combat Rules

* **Health (3 HP):**
  * `3 HP`: **Healthy 🤠**
  * `1-2 HP`: **Wounded 🩸** (First 2 gunshot hits deal 1 damage each)
  * `0 HP`: **Eliminated 💀** (3rd gunshot hit or Minotaur encounter eliminates player)
* **Hospital Healing (🏥):** Visiting the Hospital cell fully cures **Wounded 🩸** players back to **Healthy 🤠 (3 HP)**.
* **Arsenal Reloading (⚔️):** Visiting the Arsenal cell fully restocks **Bullets (3/3 🔫)** and **Grenades (3/3 💣)**.
* **The Monster (Minotaur 👹):** After each complete round of player turns, the Minotaur steps 1 cell closer to the nearest explorer. Eliminates any player it catches.
* **Stealing Treasure:** If an explorer carrying the treasure is eliminated, the treasure drops in that cell for others to claim.

---

## 🏆 Winning Conditions
A player wins if they:
1. Find the **treasure** (or take it from a rival).
2. Carry the treasure to an **Exit** cell and successfully leave the labyrinth.

*(In a pure survival variant, the last surviving explorer wins).*
